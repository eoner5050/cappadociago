CappadociaGo Admin Tour Panel - No OpenAI / Existing Tour Revision Merge
2026-08-30

- Existing CappadociaGo tours remain listed in /admin/tours.
- Existing tours can be opened and revised without changing their live URL.
- Revision can be saved as draft or published.
- Existing-site revisions can be reverted to the original static tour.
